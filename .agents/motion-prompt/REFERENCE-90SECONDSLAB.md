# Reference study — @90secondslab reel `Deb39i_PLRE` (eFishery)

Studied 2026-10-19. Source: `https://www.instagram.com/p/Deb39i_PLRE/`
Local evidence: `refs/90sl_0s.jpg` … `refs/90sl_62s.jpg` (frames at 0/1/2/3/30/62s).

Measured file: 720x1280, 30 fps, h264+aac, **84.68 s**. Uploaded at 720p, not 1080p.

Language is Indonesian. That is a Reel, not a Short — it breaks the 60s Shorts ceiling on purpose.
Adopt the mechanism, not the language, not the duration.

## 1. Hook structure (the part worth stealing)

Three beats, one per second, no narration required to understand it.

| t | Visual enters | Text block enters |
|---|---|---|
| 0s | Open pop-up book on wood desk + founder cutout. No text. | — |
| 1s | Brand signpost pops up beside the subject | `PETERNAK LELE` — white on charcoal |
| 2s | Three catfish cutouts pop in, scattered diagonal | `JAGO NGARANG?` — black on yellow |
| 3s | Camera holds | `KOK BISA?` — charcoal on white |

Rules the hook actually follows:

1. **Subject first, text second.** The frame is legible and interesting for one full second with zero words.
2. **One line per second, stacked downward.** Never a paragraph, never two lines at once.
3. **Alternating colour blocks**, not one banner: dark → yellow → white. The alternation is what makes the ladder read as three separate thoughts.
4. **Every block tilted ~-2°**, hand-placed look. Straight boxes read as a slide deck.
5. **Ultra-condensed ultra-bold caps**, hard drop shadow (offset +8/+10px, zero blur). Condensed caps at that weight survive phone recompression; thin type does not.
6. **One prop per text line.** Copy and object arrive together, so the line has a referent.
7. **The last line is the question the viewer is already thinking.** `KOK BISA?` = "how is that possible". It closes the loop by naming it, not by answering.

## 2. Three recurring layout templates

| Template | Use | Build spec |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Pop-up book on desk** | Narrative scene, characters, props | Warm wall `#E5DFD5` top 35%, oak desk `#C8A882` below; open book centred y 38–73%; cutouts carry a 6px white die-cut outline + soft shadow |
| **B. Top-down flat lay** | Evidence: documents, stamps, verdicts | Pure overhead, single light raking from upper left; objects rotated 3–8° off axis; one red-ink stamp for the verdict beat |
| **C. Dot-grid metric canvas** | Numbers, comparisons | Off-white `#F4F1EA` with a faint dot grid; no chrome, no panel, no card — the number IS the layout |

Note template C: there is **no dashboard**. No rounded-rect KPI cards, no gauges with axes. The metric sits raw on paper and is annotated by hand.

## 3. Number treatment (measured from the 30s frame)

- Fill `#FFC822`, black stroke 6px, hard shadow offset +8/+10, zero blur.
- Width ~68% of canvas, cap height ~9% of frame height.
- Conversion line (`US$1,4 miliar`) in plain black bold, ~42px, tucked under the metric.
- Qualifier badge as a **stamped pill**: black `#121212`, white caps ~50px, rotated -3.5°.

## 4. How quantities were shown without charts

- **Beakers of cash** — vertical glass cylinders filled with Rp100k notes = runway in months.
- **Waffle grid** — 400 yellow squares vs 5 blocks = claimed vs real units. One glance, no axis.
- **Two bars only** — `Rp12 T` (claimed) towering over `Rp2,5 T` (real), labels inside the bars.

Lesson: physicalise the quantity. A counter ticking to 94,000 is weaker than 94 hulls you can count in rows.

## 5. Captions

- Bottom safe zone, y 78–86% (≈ bottom margin 270px at 1920).
- Rounded bold geometric sans ~52px line height, centred.
- White `#FFFFFF` with `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` soft shadow.
- **Karaoke word highlight**: current word gets a solid yellow `#FFC822` marker block behind it, white text on top.
- Handle watermark bottom right, white at 60% opacity.

## 6. Edit rhythm

- Static/identification shot: ~1.0–1.5 s.
- Narration beat: ~1.0–2.0 s per phrase.
- Transition: 0.2–0.4 s **paper slide wipe** — a sheet slides across to reveal the layer underneath. Not a cross-dissolve, not a hard cut. Cheap to fake in SVG: an oversized rotated rect with a paper texture sliding over the stage.

## 7. What transfers to @IRinANutshell, and what does not

Transfer:
- The hook ladder (one line/second, alternating blocks, tilted, subject-first).
- Physicalise quantities instead of counters.
- Paper-slide wipe as the beat transition.
- No dashboard chrome; raw type on a flat ground.

Do not transfer:
- Indonesian copy — channel mandate is English-only.
- 85s runtime — Shorts ceiling is 60s; IR target stays 40–48s.
- Papercraft/desk aesthetic as the *only* stage — IR's anchor is a vector geopolitical map. The ladder and the type treatment sit on top of the map, the map does not get replaced.
- Consumer-shock framing (`JAGO NGARANG?`). IR hook must name a structural question, not accuse a company.
