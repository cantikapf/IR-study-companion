Situation

Summer 1914: Europe's balance of power rests on a knife-edge between two rigid alliance blocs: the Triple Entente (Britain, France, Russia) and the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary). Under realist theory, systemic stability requires rough power equilibrium.

Your Role

You are the Great Power Equilibrium Coordinator. Across 4 sequential crisis shocks in July 1914, you must reallocate diplomatic counterweights to prevent the power imbalance from exceeding the critical 20-point rupture threshold.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Equilibrium Keeper: Survive all 4 crises with final power margin ≤ 10.
  • ★★ Armed Stabilizer: Survive all 4 crises with margin between 11 and 20.
  • 0–1★ July 1914 Cascade: Power margin exceeds 20 points twice, triggering chain-ganging World War I.

How It Works

  • 4 Historical Crisis Shocks: Sarajevo Assassination → Blank Check → Russian Mobilization → Belgian Neutrality.
  • 2 Reallocation Points: After each shock, counterweight either bloc (+6 pts) or deploy neutralizing diplomacy (−6 margin).
  • SIGINT Cable: Confidential embassy decrypt in Crisis 2 hints at Italian bloc realignment.
