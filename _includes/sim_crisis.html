Crisis Command: Thirteen Days

≈ 4 min 3 acts under pressure 12s DEFCON clock Foundation level

Situation

October 1962: U-2 spy plane reconnaissance reveals Soviet medium-range nuclear ballistic missiles under construction in Cuba, 90 miles from Florida. The missiles could strike Washington D.C. in three minutes.

Your Role

You are US President John F. Kennedy directing the Executive Committee of the National Security Council (ExComm). You must make 3 high-stakes decisions to defuse the standoff without triggering nuclear war.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Master Strategist (85+ Stability): Reach a peaceful compromise deal while preserving alliance credibility.
  • ★★ Crisis Manager (70–84 Stability): Avert war with significant diplomatic friction or concessions.
  • ★ Beleaguered President (40–69 Stability): Avoid nuclear exchange but suffer severe geopolitical loss.
  • 0★ Catastrophe (Stability < 40 or War): Full nuclear exchange or humiliating capitulation.

How It Works

  • Stability starts at 100: Escalatory actions and military missteps deplete stability rapidly.
  • 12s DEFCON Clock: Each act grants 12 seconds to decide before panic indecision penalizes Stability by −10.
  • Intelligence Intercepts: Intercept tactical SIGINT reports between acts to expose Soviet red lines.
