🎯 Game Objective

Survive 8 rounds against a secret opponent and finish with the highest score you can. ★1+ Survivor · ★★ 15+ Negotiator · ★★★ 40+ Master Diplomat. The golden path: chain mutual-cooperation streaks (×1.5 at 3 in a row, ×2 at 5) without getting betrayed. Finish negative and you were exploited. Beat your saved Best.

⚖️ Payoff Matrix (You / Opponent)

Rival Cooperates Rival Defects
You Cooperate +5 / +5 −10 / +10
You Defect +10 / −10 −2 / −2

Mutual cooperation pays best over time — but every round, defection tempts you with +10. That tension is the Prisoner's Dilemma.

📖 How to Play

  • 1. Begin Session: Press Begin Session below — one of 5 secret strategies is drawn for your rival.
  • 2. Make Your Move: Each round pick Cooperate or Defect — click the buttons or press C / D.
  • 3. Simultaneous Reveal: Moves reveal at once; payoffs apply. Read the situation log under the arena.
  • 4. Read the Tells: Study the rival's answers — repeating patterns leak their hidden strategy.
  • 5. SIGINT Intercept: After Round 4, a SIGINT intel hint drops. Use it to plan your endgame.
  • 6. Debrief & Rank: After Round 8, reveal the rival's doctrine, receive stars and rank, then replay.
