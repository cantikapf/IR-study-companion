Lab 02 Strategy · Game Theory

The Prisoner's Dilemma

≈ 4 min 8-round sessions Foundation level

Situation

You are State A. Across the table sits State B, an AI power whose strategy is classified. Each round, both states secretly choose: Cooperate (keep weapons banned) or Defect (secretly build). Choices are revealed simultaneously.

Payoff Matrix

B Cooperates B Defects
You Cooperate +5 / +5 −10 / +10
You Defect +10 / −10 −2 / −2

Objectives

  • Maximize your cumulative score across an 8-round session.
  • There is no single winning move — the rational choice depends on how your opponent reacts over time. Discover its pattern.
  • End with a negative score and you have been systematically exploited.

How It Works

  • Mutual cooperation is the joint best outcome (+5 each) — but each state is tempted to defect for +10.
  • State B adapts to your history. Watch how it answers each of your moves before you commit to a pattern.
  • The one-shot game favors defection; the iterated game rewards a different logic. Eight rounds is enough to see which.
