Second-Strike Ledger: The Nuclear Triad Trial

≈ 3 min 4 Fiscal Years Triad Survivability Stress-Test Strategy level

Situation

Under nuclear deterrence theory and Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD), strategic stability does not depend on the number of warheads you possess, but on whether your retaliatory force can survive an adversary's surprise first strike.

Your Role

You are Director of Strategic Deterrence Planning. Across 4 Fiscal Years (FY1–FY4), you must allocate 100 strategic budget units across the nuclear triad and missile defense to withstand adversary counterforce stress-tests.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Triad Architect: Maintain strategic stability across all 4 fiscal years with ≥2 triad legs survivable.
  • ★★ Deterrence Caretaker: Maintain stability across 4 years with 1 survivable leg.
  • 0–1★ Preemptive Rupture: Stability collapses below 25%, opening a first-strike window.

How It Works

  • Triad Procurement: Balance land-based ICBMs (readiness), submarine SLBMs (stealth survivability), bombers (recallable signaling), and missile defense.
  • Counterforce Stress-Tests: Each fiscal year ends with an adversarial crisis probing your weakest leg.
  • The Defense Paradox: Excessive investment in Ballistic Missile Defense (BMD) alarms the adversary, accelerating offensive arms spirals.
