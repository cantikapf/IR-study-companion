Chair's Gambit: ASEAN South China Sea Summit

≈ 3 min 3 negotiation rounds 6 faction meters Strategy level

Situation

You assume the rotating Chairmanship of ASEAN at a critical summit. Maritime incidents in the South China Sea have brought coastal claimants and great powers to the brink of crisis. All 10 member states look to you to finalize a binding Code of Conduct (COC).

Your Role

You are the ASEAN Chair diplomat. You must steer the summit across 3 contentious drafting rounds while juggling 6 competing faction interests to preserve ASEAN Centrality.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Master Diplomat: COC adopted with all 6 faction meters ≥ 30%.
  • ★★ Consensus Broker: COC adopted with moderate compromises (some meters < 30%).
  • 0–1★ Communique Collapse: Any faction meter hits zero or consensus breaks (repeating Phnom Penh 2012).

How It Works

  • 6 Faction Meters: Claimants, Non-Claimants, China, Dialogue Partners, ASEAN Unity, and Agreement Substance.
  • Consensus Survival: The ASEAN Way demands unanimous consensus (Musyawarah-Mufakat). Pushing one faction too hard triggers vetoes.
  • SIGINT Chair Note: Confidentially decrypt non-claimant diplomatic red lines after Round 1.
