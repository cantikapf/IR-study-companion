Situation

Two states — Alpha and Beta — live in international anarchy without a supreme authority. Both start with equal militaries (power 5 each) and moderate mutual distrust (threat perception 50% each).

Your Role

You direct State Alpha's grand strategy across 5 rounds. State Beta is an AI opponent with a secret doctrinal posture: Cautious Reactive or Opportunistic.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Security Community: End with both threat perceptions below 35%.

How It Works

  • Build: +2 Military — triggers +20 Beta threat.
  • Signal & Reduce: Signal cuts Beta threat by 10; Reduce cuts your power by 1 and threat by 15.
  • CBM Restraint Momentum: Chaining non-Build rounds unleashes bonus threat reduction (−5 from Round 3).
