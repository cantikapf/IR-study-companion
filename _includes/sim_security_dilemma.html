The Security Dilemma Spiral

≈ 3 min 5 decisions Foundation level

Situation

Two states — Alpha and Beta — live in anarchy: there is no world police above them. Both start with equal militaries (power 5 each) and moderate distrust (threat perception 50% on both sides).

Your Role

You direct State Alpha's grand strategy for 5 rounds. State Beta is an AI government reacting cautiously to every move you make.

Objectives

  • Security Community: end the simulation with both threat perceptions below 35%.
  • Uneasy Détente: both perceptions stay between 35% and 75%.
  • Arms Race Spiral: either perception rises above 75%. Avoid this.

How It Works

  • Build adds +2 to your military — and raises Beta's threat perception by 20.
  • Signal offers diplomacy (Beta's threat −10); Reduce cuts your military by 1 (Beta's threat −15).
  • Beta reacts to your last move each round. Watch its response pattern before committing.
