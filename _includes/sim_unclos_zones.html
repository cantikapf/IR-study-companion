Situation

The 1982 UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) divides the world's oceans into distinct legal maritime zones, radiating outward from coastal baselines to the High Seas and the international Area. Sovereign rights attenuate with distance.

Your Role

You are Legal Counsel to the Naval Maritime Command. You must classify 10 rapid jurisdictional incidents correctly to enforce law without causing international diplomatic ruptures.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Maritime Admiral: Clear all 10 zones with all 3 Incident Tokens intact.
  • ★★ Fleet Commander: Clear all 10 zones with 2 Tokens remaining.
  • ★ Coastal Patrolman: Clear all 10 zones with 1 Token remaining.
  • 0★ Navigational Rupture: Exhaust all 3 tokens before finishing.

How It Works

  • 3 Incident Tokens: Every misclassification burns 1 Token and breaks your combo.
  • ×1.5 Combo Multiplier: Consecutive correct answers stack a multiplier on difficulty points.
  • SIGINT Satellite Pass: Use your one-time Satellite Tactical Advisory pass to safely bypass any single hard incident.
