Veto Gauntlet: UNSC Resolution Chamber

≈ 3 min 15 Council Members 5 Amendment Tokens Strategy level

Situation

An armed conflict has erupted in a contested regional corridor, threatening hundreds of thousands of civilians. The United Nations Security Council is convened in emergency session to draft a binding humanitarian ceasefire resolution.

Your Role

You are the "Penholder" delegation holding the Council Presidency. You must attach amendment tokens to the resolution text to balance great-power red lines without gutting the support of the Elected Ten (E10).

Objectives

  • ★★★ Unanimous Mandate: 15 affirmative votes with zero vetoes.
  • ★★ Council Consensus: 9–14 affirmative votes with zero vetoes.
  • ★ Bare Quorum: 6–8 votes (Fails adoption, lacks Article 27 nine-vote supermajority).
  • 0★ Veto Paralysis: Any single P5 permanent member casts a negative vote.

How It Works

  • Article 27(3) Rule: Adoption requires 9 affirmative votes including the concurring votes of all five permanent members (P5). A single P5 "NO" kills the resolution instantly.
  • 5 Amendment Tokens: Select amendments to pacify hidden P5 demands. But be careful: watering down the text too far causes the E10 to abstain.
  • SIGINT Aide-Mémoire: Review confidential intelligence regarding P5 core thresholds.
