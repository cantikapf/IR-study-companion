Lab 03 CASE FILE · GLOBAL TRADE & GOVERNANCE

Consensus Market: The Doha Round Trap

≈ 3 min 8 voting blocs 10 Capital Points Strategy level

Situation

The World Trade Organization (WTO) operates under the principle of absolute consensus: unlike majority-voting bodies, any single member can veto a multilateral treaty. Under the Single Undertaking doctrine, "nothing is agreed until everything is agreed."

Your Role

You are the WTO Director-General brokering a comprehensive package deal among 8 contentious member coalitions with conflicting national red lines. You hold 10 Diplomatic Capital points to fund concessions.

Objectives

  • ★★★ Master Facilitator: Pass consensus treaty with ≥4 Diplomatic Capital remaining.
  • ★★ Diplomatic Broker: Pass consensus treaty with 1–3 Capital remaining.
  • ★ Exhausted Signatory: Pass consensus with exactly 0 Capital left.
  • 0★ Ministerial Collapse: Any single member bloc exercises its veto.

How It Works

  • Budgeted Concessions: Allocate 10 Diplomatic Capital across 5 treaty pillars (costs 0–3 each).
  • Hidden Red Lines: Blocs conceal their true reservation prices. Over-conceding wastes capital; under-conceding triggers vetoes.
  • SIGINT Leak: An intelligence report reveals whether a pivotal agricultural bloc is bluffing.
