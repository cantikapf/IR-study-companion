    <h2>Mission & Academic Philosophy</h2>
    <p>
      The study of International Relations is too often locked behind expensive university paywalls, dense and dry anthologies, or fragmented online articles that lack pedagogical structure. <strong>IR Study Companion</strong> was created to deliver a comprehensive, university-grade curriculum that is 100% open-access, interactive, and grounded in rigorous scholarship.
    </p>
    <p>
      Our curriculum spans <strong>18 core academic modules</strong> and <strong>157 structured lessons</strong>, covering the full breadth of the discipline: from foundational debates on anarchy and state sovereignty to modern cyber warfare, the political economy of development, public international law, ASEAN regionalism, and Indonesian foreign policy.
    </p>

    <div style="background: var(--lms-subtle); border-left: 3px solid var(--lms-ink-primary); padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem; border-radius: 0 var(--lms-radius-md) var(--lms-radius-md) 0; margin: 2rem 0;">
      <strong>Core Institutional Focus:</strong> Providing an interactive, distraction-free, and QA-verified digital learning companion that equips students and practitioners with the theoretical tools to analyze world politics objectively.
    </div>

    <h2>Editorial & Peer-Review Methodology</h2>
    <p>
      Academic integrity is the cornerstone of this platform. Every lesson adheres to strict pedagogical standards:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Verified Scholarly Literature:</strong> Centralized bibliographies are cross-checked against CrossRef REST APIs and official treaty depositories (UN Treaty Series, ICJ judgments, WTO appellate reports, and ASEAN legal instruments).</li>
      <li><strong>Substantive Factual Auditing:</strong> The platform undergoes continuous empirical audits, ensuring theoretical accuracy (such as formal game-theoretic payoffs in Prisoner's Dilemma, Putnam's two-level game win-sets, and precise diplomatic treaty dates).</li>
      <li><strong>Zero 'Yes-Man' Policy:</strong> Content avoids superficial soundbites, presenting competing theoretical paradigms (Realism, Liberalism, Constructivism, Marxism, and Postcolonialism) with equal academic rigor.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Pedagogical Architecture</h2>
    <p>
      Unlike passive reading websites, IR Study Companion employs an active-learning architecture:
    </p>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Focus-Mode Reading:</strong> A clean, distraction-free reading experience with chapter outlines and estimated reading times to maximize focus and retention.</li>
      <li><strong>Knowledge Checkpoints:</strong> Formative multiple-choice questions at the end of each lesson provide immediate scholarly rationale and direct links back to cited passages.</li>
      <li><strong>Summative Module Examinations:</strong> 18 rigorous 10-question exams (180 questions total) requiring a 70% mastery threshold to earn academic certification credit.</li>
      <li><strong>Interactive Diplomatic Labs:</strong> 10 bespoke simulation engines built in vanilla ES6—including the Cuban Missile Crisis, UN Security Council P5 Veto, UNCLOS Maritime Zoning, and ASEAN South China Sea negotiations.</li>
    </ol>
