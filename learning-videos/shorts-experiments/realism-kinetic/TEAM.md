# Team & Task Graph — Realism YouTube Shorts (Kinetic Style)

## Team Roles

- `director`: Project orchestrator, verifies requirements, supervises gates, issues final clearance.
- `screenwriter`: Refines hook, ensures pacing fits 43–45s spoken English at ~135 WPM.
- `audio-engineer`: Generates Kokoro-82M speech audio and aligns millisecond sentence timestamps.
- `motion-designer`: Implements Remotion TSX kinetic motion components in vertical 9:16 format with strict safe zones.
- `reviewer`: Audits preview still frames for safe zone violations, contrast, readability, and pacing.
