## 2. Why the structure works (mechanism, not vibe)

1. **A prop introduces every idea before a word says it.** Cash stack, clock, two books, waffle grid, gauge. No concept arrives as a bare sentence.
2. **One number per shot, always with its unit and its source.** `Rp12 T (US$752 juta)` carries `menurut draf investigasi` on the same card.
3. **Comparisons reuse the same object.** 400,000 vs 5,000 uses one grid; 12 T vs 2.5 T uses two bars of the same scale. No axis, no legend.
4. **The objection is voiced, then answered.** `NGGAK NGECEK? NGECEK. TAPI...` — the viewer's counter-argument appears as a title card and gets resolved.
5. **The ending returns to the subject's own product.** The feeder that meters fish feed is used to overfeed a book. The metaphor is the argument.
6. **Transitions are physical.** Paper slides, book opens, page flips. Nothing cross-dissolves.

## 3. What does NOT transfer

- 85 s and 21 shots — our target is a Short; the ceiling is 60 s.
- Indonesian copy — channel mandate is English-only.
- Accusatory framing toward a named living person — IR content argues about structure, not culpability.
- Karaoke word-highlight subtitles — reserved for narrated long-form, not this.

## 4. What does transfer, concretely

| Mechanism | Our version |
|---|---|
| Prop introduces the idea first | Gauge between two coastlines before the words "1.5 nautical miles" |
| One number, unit, source on the card | `1.5 NM · 2.8 KM` with `Phillips Channel` named on the same card |
| Reuse one object for the comparison | Same water column: 20.5 m hull vs ~25 m seabed → 4.5 m clearance |
| Voice the objection, then answer it | `WHY NOT JUST GO AROUND?` → then the Sunda depth limit |
| End on the subject's own logic | The strait cannot be widened — that is the whole argument |
| Physical transitions | Paper-slide wipe between beats |
