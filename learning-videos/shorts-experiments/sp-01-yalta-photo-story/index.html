HISTORICAL DOSSIER · YALTA 1945
LIVADIA PALACE, CRIMEA · FEB 9, 1945
One of these men will be dead in 60 days.
Guess which one.
Not the oldest.
The one in the middle.
Roosevelt was dying. His hands shook too badly to pour his own coffee.
Stalin was the host. Not by accident.
He demanded Eastern Europe. He got it.
Churchill won the war.
Five months later, Britain voted him out.
He called it “the ingratitude of the British people.”
Roosevelt dead by April. Churchill out by July.
Stalin ruled for eight more years.
One photo. Three empires.
