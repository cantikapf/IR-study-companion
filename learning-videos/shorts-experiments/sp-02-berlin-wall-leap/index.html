HISTORICAL DOSSIER · BERLIN 1961
CHECKPOINT CHARLIE, FRIEDRICHSTRAßE · OCT 27, 1961
These two tanks are seventy-five yards apart.
Both had orders to fire if the other moved.
October 27, 1961.
Checkpoint Charlie, Berlin.
American M-48s in the foreground.
Soviet T-55s waiting across the line.
Engines idling for 16 hours straight.
One nervous nineteen-year-old on a trigger...
would have triggered full thermonuclear war.
Behind closed doors, Kennedy and Khrushchev blinked.
One by one, the tanks backed away.
One photo. The closest humanity ever came to the brink.
