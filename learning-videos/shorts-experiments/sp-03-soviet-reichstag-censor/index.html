Soviet State Archive · May 1945
The Reichstag Negative Censor · Berlin
Before you celebrate this 1945 victory photo...
look closely at this soldier's right wrist.
May 2, 1945. The ruins of Berlin.
Red Army photographer Yevgeny Khaldei stages the iconic Reichstag flag.
Look at Sergeant Ismailov supporting the flagbearer.
On his left wrist, a watch.
On his right wrist... a second watch.
Looting was punishable by execution under Soviet military law.
Hours before publication, Khaldei scraped the second watch off the negative with a needle.
One photograph defined Soviet triumph.
A single needle stroke hid the truth for fifty years.
