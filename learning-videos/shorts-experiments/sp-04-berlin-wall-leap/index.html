ARCHIVAL DOSSIER · AUGUST 15, 1961
BERLIN WALL ESCAPE · SECTEUR FRANÇAIS
PUBLIC DOMAIN ARCHIVE · NARA / LOC CPH 3C38900
What would you do before you die if ordered to shoot escapees across the 1961 Berlin Wall?
Two days after the border closed, this nineteen-year-old soldier made his choice.
His name was Conrad Schumann.
Stationed on Bernauer Strasse, his unit was ordered to stop any defector at gunpoint.
Look closely at his hands.
He is mid-air, but he isn't aiming.
He is deliberately throwing away his loaded Soviet submachine gun.
He did it so West Berlin police wouldn't shoot him as a hostile threat.
Ten yards away, a West German police van waited with its door open.
Photographer Peter Leibing pressed the shutter at the exact millisecond.
Schumann made it to freedom.
The image embarrassed Moscow, but the Stasi hunted his family for decades.
A leap into history... paid in blood.
