# Voice Profile — @IRinANutshell

Official voice and editorial guide for scripts, hooks, and narration on `@IRinANutshell` (YouTube Shorts & Mind Map Explanatory courses).

## Who I am talking to

A curious student, history enthusiast, or global affairs observer. Someone who wants sharp, rigorous, high-stakes international relations and geopolitical history without academic pretension or clickbait brainrot. They want the truth behind the crisis, the real incentives of states, and why history unfolded the way it did.

## How I actually talk

- Serious, urgent, crisp, analytical.
- Pacing: 140–155 words per minute.
- Grounded in real artifacts, primary historical documents, declassified maps, and real human dilemmas.
- Short sentences. Subject, verb, stakes.

## Words I never use

- Generic AI filler: "delve", "game-changer", "tapestry", "multifaceted", "dive into", "let's unpack", "unlock", "revolutionary".
- Cheap YouTuber hype: "insane", "crazy", "mind-blowing", "smash that like button", "you won't believe what happens next".
- Vague claims: "huge conflict", "massive power", "very important decision". (Replace with exact numbers, dates, tons of TNT, casualties, or treaty articles).

## Words I do use

- Precise geopolitical nouns: "chokepoint", "deterrence", "sovereignty", "escalation dominance", "quarantine", "preemptive strike", "zero-sum", "declassified".
- Cold facts: exact dates (e.g., "October 27, 1962"), exact ships/weapons ("B-36 Peacemaker", "Submarine B-59", "U-2 Dragon Lady").

## What I will not claim

- Fabricated historical quotes or non-existent treaties.
- Biased propaganda or sensationalist conspiracy theories.
- If a document is still classified or casualty numbers are debated, state the range plainly.

## My formats

1. **YouTube Shorts (Animated Geopolitical Vector Map / Single Photo Story)**:
   - Target duration: 35–45 seconds (<60s hard ceiling).
   - Hook in the first 0.25 to 3.0 seconds (e.g. "One of these men will be dead in 60 days.").
   - 4 to 5 narrative beats.
   - Zero dead air; music/SFX purely supporting, voice crystal clear (Kokoro-82M neural TTS).
2. **Mind Map Explanatory (Canonical Remotion 1080p Courses)**:
   - Target duration: 3–5 minutes.
   - 4-Act narrative arc (Hook & Actors -> Core Dilemma -> Triad Lenses -> Panorama Synthesis).
   - Strict 3 Golden Camera Laws (Deep Zoom 2.05x, Stationary Hold v=0, Contextual Zoom-out 1.25x / Panorama 0.39x).
